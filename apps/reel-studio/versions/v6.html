FAQ

Short answers to common questions.

How much does it cost?

Reel Studio is free to download and use for personal projects. Team plans with shared presets and review links are coming later.

What are the system requirements?

macOS 12 or later on Apple Silicon or Intel, or Linux (.deb or AppImage). 8 GB of RAM is recommended, and 16 GB for 4K projects.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Editing, markup, color grading and export all run on your computer. You only need a connection to download updates or to use AI-written motion code.

Which formats can I export?

MP4, ProRes and GIF, up to 4K at 60 frames per second. Your marks and motion are rendered into the file.

Is there a Windows version?

Windows builds are coming soon. Join the community to get notified.
